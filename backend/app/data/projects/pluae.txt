### PLUAE – University Job Platform

**Category:** Full-stack Web Application

**Description:**
A responsive university job platform connecting students and companies.

**Technologies:**
- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- PostgreSQL

**Features:**
- Multi-role dashboards
- Secure authentication
- PostgreSQL integration
- Student-company connections
- Job management
