### Smart Recitation Tracker

**Category:** AI / Speech Recognition / Quran Technology

**Description:**
An intelligent application that uses Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) to convert Quran recitation into text, compare it against the reference Quranic text, identify errors, calculate a matching percentage, and generate reports for review.

**Technologies:**
- Vosk
- Speech Recognition
- Arabic Speech Processing
- Quranic Text Processing

**Features:**
- Speech-to-text
- Quran reference comparison
- Error detection
- Matching percentage
- Recitation review reports
