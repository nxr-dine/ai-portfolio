### Wonderlust

**Category:** AI-powered Travel Platform

**Description:**
An integrated web and mobile travel platform designed for trip planning, AI-powered recommendations, and secure booking management.

**Technologies:**
- React
- Node.js
- MongoDB
- Flask

**Features:**
- Trip planning
- AI-based recommendations
- Booking management
- Web and mobile experience
