### AI Portfolio Assistant

**Category:** Full-stack Generative AI

**Description:**
An AI-powered portfolio website featuring a conversational interface, RAG-based knowledge retrieval, and real-time streaming chat. Visitors can explore projects, skills, experience, and career interests through natural conversation.

**Technologies:**
- FastAPI
- Next.js
- LangChain
- PostgreSQL
- pgvector
- Redis
- OpenAI

**Features:**
- AI chatbot with portfolio knowledge base
- Real-time SSE streaming
- Project and skills showcase
- Resume integration
- Contact form
